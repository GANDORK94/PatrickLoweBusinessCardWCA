# Patrick Lowe — Digital Business Card (Westcoast Auto Sales)

A digital business card I built while working at Westcoast Auto Sales. Navy and gold luxury aesthetic, smooth animations, and a one-tap save-to-contacts button.

---

## My Thought Process

I was in automotive sales and wanted a digital business card that actually matched the brand of a luxury dealership. Paper cards feel cheap. I wanted something that looked premium — the kind of thing you'd actually remember scanning.

I built this with Claude AI. No coding background, just a clear vision of the look and feel I wanted and enough back-and-forth to get it there. Navy, gold, elegant typography. Something that said "luxury" without being over the top.

### What I wanted

A card that:
- Looked the part for a high-end auto brand
- Loaded fast on any phone
- Made it dead simple to save my contact info

The "Save to Contacts" button downloads the contact directly — name, title, phone, email — no typing required on the customer's end. In sales, anything that reduces friction is a win.

---

## Features

- Luxury navy and gold design with smooth fade-in animations
- Clickable contact links — phone, email, and more
- One-tap **Save to Contacts** button
- Mobile optimized — built for how customers actually use their phones
